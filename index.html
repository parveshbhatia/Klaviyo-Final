Inside Klaviyo · 02
The trigger

Triggering on Viewed Product

  • Set the trigger to the metric "Viewed Product".
  • It's fed by Klaviyo's onsite tracking for known profiles.
  • Add flow filters so it won't overlap other flows.
  • That single event is what powers browse re-engagement.
